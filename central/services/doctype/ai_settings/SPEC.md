# AI

## Purpose

Central sells AI served by Grove. A team with AI is one Central Team at Grove, named by the team id. Grove owns the models, the tiers (a team's key count, monthly spend limit and rate limits), the usage and the API keys, and each key carries its own policy: the geography it works in, the models it may call, its rate limits, and the cap it may spend each month. Central keeps no key: a key's secret is shown once, in the answer that mints it.

## Configuration

AI Settings holds the Grove URL and Central's control credential. A System Manager sets **Grove URL** and selects **Enroll** with the bootstrap secret Grove was given. Grove mints Central's control user and key (`grove.api.control.create_control_client`), and Central stores them. Enroll is a plain whitelisted call, not a form method, so the secret arrives at the top of the request and is popped before anything logs it. **Rotate Credential** asks Grove for a new secret under the current one (`grove.api.control.create_control_client_key`); the old one stops at once.

## Operation

- **Turning AI on**: `central.services.api.ai.enable_ai(team)` inserts a Team Service with `add_on_service = "ai"`. Its `before_insert` checks the team has no AI yet, then registers it at Grove by its id alone (`provision_team`; Grove keeps no email or contact) and sends its limits (`update_team`, below), so a refusal there leaves no row. Every team is billed by Central, so it is paid and gated at Grove. `enable` locks the Team row, so two enables at once make one service. An `ai` row has no region, no subscription and no endpoint: each key calls its own geography's gateway. A team has one.
- **Keys**: `list_api_keys`, `create_api_key`, `set_api_key_caps` and `revoke_api_key` pass through to Grove with `team`. Only `create_api_key` returns a secret. A key is minted in a geography (one of `get_ai().geographies` with an `endpoint`; a geography has none until its gateway is up, and Grove refuses to mint there; Grove's default when none is picked), with a monthly cap in whole US dollars (Central refuses a fraction; Grove stores what it is given); minted without one, it starts at 0 and its gateway refuses it until `set_api_key_caps` gives it one. The geography never changes: a team spans geographies by minting a key in each. `set_api_key_caps` sets several keys' caps in one call (the console's **Set monthly caps**, one row per live key); Grove saves lowers first and all or none, refusing caps that, with what the team's keys already spent this month (revoked ones too), would add up past its tier's monthly spend limit, so the keys together never outspend the tier and a spent cap frees nothing when moved or its key revoked. Grove reads what its gateways hold of the team before it checks, and refuses a raise while a gateway store cannot be read. The console shows spend as of Grove's last pull (the usage `as_of`) and does not refresh before a change: Grove's refusal names the live figure. A key at its cap is refused until the month ends or the cap is raised. Each key runs under the rate limits of its team's tier at Grove (Grove's default tier for an untiered team) unless Grove pins it its own, and calls the models decided at Grove. Grove refuses a key another team holds, refuses to revoke a key younger than six hours (it lists each key's `revocable_at`, and the console holds Revoke back until then rather than asking), and holds a team to the key count of its tier at Grove (0 is no limit).
- **Models**: `get_models(team, geography)` lists what a key in a geography starts with (the published models of Grove's default group there), for the overview and the keys tab's **How to call** (a geography picker, that geography's base URL and its models); `create_api_key` answers with what the new key may call.
- **Tier limits and standing**: Grove holds a `Tier` per trust tier name with the AI key count (0 is no limit), the monthly spend limit (USD) and the rate limits a team's keys run under, plus a per-team override and a default tier for an untiered team; the values are Grove's to edit. `services.ai.get_team_limits` is what Central tells Grove: the level's **Tier** name (through `get_team_caps`; untiered: "", Grove's default tier) and `observed_at`, the Billing Profile's `modified`. A name Grove has not seen is created there as a copy of its default tier. On enable `update_team` sends it inline, so a refusal leaves AI off. After that it travels by a Frappe **Webhook**, "Grove Team Tier", which AI Settings creates and keeps in step on every save and on migrate (`sync_webhook`): on `Billing Profile.on_update` when the level is set, changed or cleared, the body `get_team_limits` (a `jinja` hook method, rendered when the delivery runs) signed with the control secret (`X-Frappe-Webhook-Signature`, the control `api_key` in `X-Grove-Control-Key`), delivered after commit to `grove.api.teams.team_webhook`, retried on Frappe's schedule (5m, 30m, 2h, 5h, 10h…, 8 times, about 48 hours) and logged in Webhook Request Log. Grove ignores a team it does not know (AI off) and a body not newer than its last applied, so a retried or reordered delivery never overwrites a newer one. A level's renamed label is a direct `rename_tier` inside the level's save (`on_level_update`; a Webhook body carries no old value), which fails the save while Grove is down. Suspension is not sent yet: which lever suspends a team's keys (a Subscription's standing, the Team's status, or a team-level standing dunning does not have) is undecided. A lowered limit stops no key: Grove refuses only new caps and raises until the team fits.
- **Prompt logging**: Grove holds whether its gateways store the prompts and outputs of a team's keys for a short time, so support can look into a request. It is on by default. `get_ai` reads it as `spend.log_payloads` off the figures. `set_prompt_logging(team, enabled)` needs `service:manage` and sends it to Grove by `update_team`, which saves it on the team and pushes it to every key of the team. Central keeps no copy. The console shows it on the **Settings** tab.
- **Spend, usage**: `get_ai` carries the team's spend from Grove (`grove.api.usage.figures`, one read that also carries the keys and this month's usage, kept at Grove as one entry until a record lands, a correction is resolved, a key or the team is saved or its tier is edited, 30 minutes at most, so every page shows one moment) — this month's; `capped`, Σ the live caps, and `committed`, what this month has spoken for of `spend_limit` (each live key's cap or its spend, whichever is more, plus revoked keys' spend; the figure a raise is checked against); `max_keys`; `suspended`; `log_payloads` — and this month's requests, tokens and cost off the same read. `list_api_keys` reads the keys off it too. `get_usage` reads a period from Grove; usage of one key is narrowed by the key's hash, which Grove lists with it. **Refresh usage** (`pull_usage`) asks Grove to pull the team's gateways now; Grove answers the figures read afresh after it, so the reads that follow are of this moment. Grove allows 2 an hour per team, then refuses with a 429.
- **Billing and credit**: AI is postpaid and Grove holds no money. Credit is the team's Credit Wallet here, used before the card like for every other line. The month-end AI invoice line is not wired into Central's invoicing yet. When it is, it reads what landed at Grove since the last invoice, `grove.api.usage.usage(pulled_after, pulled_until)` in UTC (Central converts its invoice period, and keeps `as_of` as the next watermark), so a drain Grove pulls late goes on the next invoice and nothing is billed twice; Grove's Usage Records and resolved corrections are the ledger, and the invoice line is the only stored monthly figure.

`central.integrations.grove.GroveClient` and the "Grove Team Tier" Webhook are the only callers of Grove.
