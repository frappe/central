# Contact support

The support dialog in the console searches help articles and the tickets of your team, and sends a ticket to Frappe Cloud support. Every member of a team can use it.

## Open the support dialog

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Click **Help**, then **Contact support**.

You can also click **Contact support** in the **Actions** menu of a server. The dialog then knows the server and its site, and adds them to the ticket.

<Screenshot name="help/support-search" alt="The Get help dialog. The search box holds the text site is slow after update. Below are two help articles and one ticket of the team." />

## Search for an answer

1. In **Get help**, type what is wrong. Use 3 characters or more.
2. Read the results. **Help articles** open in a new tab. **Your team's tickets** shows tickets on the same subject.

If no result helps, click **Still need help?** or **Contact support**. The dialog keeps your text and puts it in the ticket.

If a server or a site is down, click **Report an outage**. The form opens with the correct topic and issue.

## Send a ticket

<Clip name="help/support-ticket" alt="A recording of the support dialog opened from the erp-production server. Still need help is clicked. The form shows the topic Server and the server. The issue Slow performance or high load is selected, details are typed, and the pointer moves to Send to support." />

1. Select a **Topic**: **Server**, **Site & app**, **Billing & plans**, or **Account & team**.
2. Select the **Issue**.
3. For a server or a site problem, select the **Server** and the **Site**. Leave them empty if the problem is not about one server. The dialog fills these fields when you open it from a server.
4. In **Details for support**, write what you did, what you expected, and the error that you saw.
5. Click **Send to support**.

The dialog shows **Your tickets** with the new ticket in the list.

Support sees these issues first:

| Topic | Issue |
| --- | --- |
| **Server** | Unreachable or down |
| **Site & app** | Site setup or provisioning failed |
| **Billing & plans** | Payment method or failed charge |

You can send 10 tickets in an hour. The form has no field for files.

## Follow your tickets

Open the support dialog. Click **Your tickets**.

<Screenshot name="help/support-tickets" alt="The Your tickets dialog. Three open tickets show their subject, number, age, and status. One ticket shows that a teammate raised it." />

The list shows the open tickets of your team, newest first. It holds at most 50 tickets. A ticket leaves the list when support resolves it.

Click a ticket that you raised to open it and reply. You can see the tickets of your teammates, but you cannot open them.
