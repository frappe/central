# Profile and theme

Your profile is how you appear to every team you belong to. Change your name and photo in **Settings > Profile**, and the console theme in **Settings > Preferences**. You can also delete your account in **Settings > Profile**.

## Open settings

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Settings**. The settings dialog opens on **Profile**. You can also press `⌘ ,` on macOS or `Ctrl ,` on Windows and Linux.

<Screenshot name="account/user-menu" alt="The account menu at the bottom of the sidebar with Settings, Theme, and Sign out" />

On a phone, settings open as pages instead of a dialog.

## Change your name

1. Open **Settings > Profile**.
2. Change **Full name**.
3. Click **Save**, or press Enter.

<Screenshot name="account/profile" alt="The Profile tab with the photo, the Full name field, and the Email field" />

Your email is shown but you cannot change it. It is the address you sign in with and the address invitations go to.

## Change your photo

1. Open **Settings > Profile**.
2. Click your photo under **Photo**.
3. Click **Upload a photo** and select an image. To remove the photo, click **Remove photo**.

Your teammates see the new photo in the member list. Without a photo, the console shows the first letter of your name.

## Change the theme

1. Open **Settings > Preferences**.
2. Under **Theme**, click **System**, **Light**, or **Dark**.

<Screenshot name="account/preferences" alt="The Preferences tab with System, Light, and Dark theme options" />

**System** follows the light or dark setting of your device. The change applies at once.

You can also click your name at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Theme**. The theme is saved in your browser, so set it again on each browser you use.

## Delete your account

1. Open **Settings > Profile**.
2. Next to **Delete account**, click **Delete**.
3. Do the items in the list, if there are any. Each item has a button that opens the page where you do it.
4. Type your email address, then click **Delete account**.
5. Open the email that we send you and click the link to confirm.

Your account is deleted 3 days after you click **Delete account**. Until then, you can sign in and click **Cancel deletion** in **Settings > Profile**.

### What you must do first

Each team that you own is archived with your account. Before you can delete your account, each of these teams must be ready to archive:

| Item | What to do |
|---|---|
| Servers | Terminate each server. |
| Snapshots, buckets and other add-ons | Delete them, so that they stop billing. |
| Invoices | Pay each unpaid invoice. |
| This month's charges | Add a payment method, so that we can collect the charges. |
| Other members | Transfer ownership to a member, or remove the members. |
| A suspended team | Contact support to make the team active again. |

The checks run again when the account is deleted. If you add a server during the 3 days, the deletion stops. To delete your account then, do the items and start again.

### What is deleted and what is kept

- We delete your name, your email address and your preferences, and we remove you from each team. You cannot sign in again.
- We archive each team that you own. The credit of these teams is lost.
- We keep invoices and payments, because the law requires them. They no longer show your name or email address.
