# Notifications

Frappe Cloud tells you about events on your team, such as a failed server or an upcoming payment. You get notifications by email and in the console. You choose which categories you get on each channel.

## Notification categories

| Category | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| Billing | Payments, invoices, credit balance, and mandates. |
| Servers | Server lifecycle, resizes, and health. |
| Team | Invitations, role changes, and membership. |
| Trial | When a free trial ends, stops, and is deleted. |

## Read notifications in the console

Click **Notifications** in the sidebar. The number next to it is the count of unread notifications.

<Screenshot name="account/notifications-panel" alt="The Notifications panel with two unread notifications: a failed server and an upcoming auto-payment" />

- Click **Unread** to show only unread notifications. Click **All** to show all of them.
- Use the **All categories** menu to show one category.
- Click a notification to mark it as read. If it links to a page, such as a server or an invoice, the page opens.
- Click the double check icon to mark all notifications as read.
- Click **Load More** at the bottom to see older notifications.

The panel shows notifications for the team you have open. Switch teams to see the notifications of another team.

## Choose what you get

1. Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **My profile**.
2. Click **Notifications**.
3. For each category, turn **Email** and **In-app** on or off.
4. Click **Save preferences**.

<Screenshot name="account/notification-settings" alt="The Notifications tab with a frequency menu for each category and a Digest time row, with Billing set to a daily digest" />

All categories are on for both channels, and email arrives instantly, until you change them. Your preferences apply only to the team you have open. Set them again on each team.

## Choose how often you get email

Each category can send email instantly, in a daily digest, or in a weekly digest. A digest is one email with all updates since the last digest. A weekly digest arrives on Monday.

1. In **Notifications**, turn **Email** on for a category.
2. Choose **Instantly**, **Daily digest**, or **Weekly digest** from the menu under the category.
3. For a digest, choose the **Digest time**. It applies to all categories of the team, and your time zone shows next to it.
4. Click **Save preferences**.

If you change a category back to **Instantly**, you get one email with the updates that were waiting.

## Choose which events send email

Click **Choose events** under a category to turn email off for single events. You still see them in the console.

Some events are critical, such as a failed payment or a server that is down. They show **Always sent**. Critical events are emailed at once, even when you choose a digest or turn **Email** off for the category.
