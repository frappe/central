# Tour the console

The console is where you manage your team's servers, members, and bill. The sidebar on the left opens each part of it.

<Screenshot name="get-started/console" alt="The console with the sidebar on the left and the Servers page on the right." />

| Sidebar item | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| Team name, at the top | The team switcher |
| **Search** | The command menu |
| **Notifications** | Recent events for the active team |
| **Servers** | The map and list of servers, and [new servers](/servers/create) |
| **Team** | [Members and roles](/team/members) |
| **Billing > Overview** | Spend, the payment method, and [limits](/billing/) |
| **Billing > Invoices** | [Monthly invoices](/billing/invoices) |
| Your name, at the bottom | Your profile, the theme, and sign out |

The sidebar shows only what your [role](/team/roles) lets you use. For example, a member without billing access does not see **Billing**.

## Switch teams

You can belong to more than one team. The console shows one team at a time.

1. Click the team name at the top of the sidebar.
2. Select a team, or click **Create team**.

<Screenshot name="get-started/team-switcher" alt="The team switcher open below the team name. It lists Contrail Coffee with a check mark, and Create team." />

## Search and run commands

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on macOS or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux. You can also click **Search**.

The command menu runs actions, such as **New server** and **Invite team member**, and opens any page. Type to filter the list. Use the arrow keys and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

<Screenshot name="get-started/search" alt="The command menu with the actions New server, Invite team member, and Switch team, followed by a list of pages." />

## Read notifications

Click **Notifications** to open the panel. It shows events for the active team, such as a failed server or an upcoming payment. The number next to **Notifications** is the count of unread events.

<Screenshot name="get-started/notifications" alt="The notifications panel. It shows Server failed: upgrade-test and Upcoming auto-payment." />

- Click **Unread** to hide events that you have read.
- Use **All categories** to show one category, such as servers or billing.

To choose which events you get by email, see [Notifications](/account/notifications).

## Change your profile and theme

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar.

<Screenshot name="get-started/user-menu" alt="The user menu open above the name Zara Quill, with My profile, Theme, and Sign out." />

- **My profile** opens your name and photo.
- **Theme** switches the console between light, dark, and your system setting.
- **Sign out** ends your session on this device.
